# v0.7.1 Phase 071-B2 reflection

## Completed slice

`OW-071-10`, `OW-071-11`, `OW-071-12`, and `OW-071-14` are complete for the
v0.7.1 development scope. The slice now provides a canonical installation
hub, lifecycle documentation, verified skill-discovery/runtime boundaries, and
bounded adoption presets.

## Evidence and QA

- OW-071-10: Windows CPython 3.12 wheel-only smoke passed; independent fixture
  QA passed after import-origin hardening. Evidence:
  `internal/owledge/reviews/ow-071-10-package-smoke-evidence.md`.
- OW-071-11: independent architecture/privacy QA passed at 97/100.
- OW-071-12: independent runtime-conformance QA passed at 96/100.
- OW-071-14: independent power-user/architecture QA passed at 100/100.

## Findings and decisions

- The original cross-platform package criterion lacked a supported local
  interpreter and remote execution evidence. A real Windows package smoke and
  portable fixture now exist.
- `D-071-24` moves actual macOS/Linux execution to the Stable/GA gate. This is
  a deferral of verification, not a cross-platform support claim.
- The CI workflow is triggered by `main` pushes or pull requests; a branch
  push alone is not execution evidence.

## Next-plan steering

`OW-071-05` is the sole next implementation ticket in wave `W-071-B3`. It may
reuse the canonical installation, workflow, integration, and preset paths but
must not broaden runtime claims or claim macOS/Linux verification. The
three-OS wheel smoke remains a mandatory `OW-100-09` Stable/GA prerequisite.

## Gate position

`G-071-B-ADOPTION` remains open until `OW-071-05` has independent beginner QA.
No later version is unlocked by this reflection.
