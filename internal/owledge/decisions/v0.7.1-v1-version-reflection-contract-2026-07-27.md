# v0.7.1-v1 Version Reflection Contract

## Decision

Treat each version bump as one product-steering phase:

1. implement only the accepted ticket scope;
2. pass the cumulative release gate;
3. compile the version review from committed evidence;
4. expose every material problem, gap, deviation, and decision to the owner;
5. reflect the proposed next-version plan against what was learned;
6. stop for an explicit `approve`, `adjust`, or `defer` decision.

This applies to v0.7.1, v0.8.0, v0.8.1, v0.9.0, and v1.0. Technical gate
success never authorizes publication, tagging, or the next version.

## Required Registers

During implementation, `RUN-STATE.yaml` carries:

- `alignment.open_questions`: unresolved owner questions;
- `alignment.findings`: problems, gaps, deviations, regressions, and new risks;
- `alignment.decisions`: decisions made or requested, with authority and status;
- `alignment.next_plan_reflection`: the proposed keep/amend/defer/drop result
  for the next version, including affected tickets and gates.

Every material finding uses a stable ID and records source ticket/gate,
evidence, severity, user impact, release impact, options, recommendation, safe
default, and status. Every decision records its related finding/question,
decider, rationale, constraints, affected artifacts, and verification status.

## Communication Contract

The version update must distinguish:

- shipped and proven behavior;
- implementation findings and their consequences;
- decisions already made within delegated authority;
- decisions that still require the owner;
- proposed changes to the next version;
- limitations and explicitly deferred work.

No finding or decision may disappear between a ticket, a gate, and the version
update. `None` is written explicitly for an empty register.

## Escalation

Ordinary reversible findings accumulate for the version review. Safety,
privacy, credentials/cost, data loss, external commitments, acceptance changes,
and irreversible architecture decisions stop immediately and require owner
direction.

## Promotion Rule

Only `approve` unlocks the next release. `adjust` creates or amends the smallest
affected ticket/gate and reruns validation. `defer` records destination,
rationale, limitation, and residual risk; it does not silently authorize
publication or next-version execution.

## Resume State

The next executable release remains v0.7.1 Phase 071-A. Start with `OW-071-01`
after control-plane validation, a clean or intentionally scoped worktree check,
and base-SHA capture. The current Handoff Decision Bridge remains a constraint
source for the mapped `OW-071-*`, `OW-080-*`, `OW-081-*`, `OW-090-*`, and
`OW-100-*` tickets.
